Day by Day

Seven days of slow alpine bliss

1
Mon 22 June

Arrival & First Evening

Land, settle in, fall for the old town

Travel
07:00Fly Heathrow → Geneva. Land 09:35, grab the rental car.
10:30The scenic drive from Geneva into Annecy — 50 minutes of mountain views, windows down. Just us and the road.
MiddayCheck in, drop bags, decompress. First swim in the pool.
AfternoonBig food shop — stock up the kitchen for the week. Good bread, charcuterie, fresh fruit, olives, picnic supplies.
SunsetEvening walk through Annecy Old Town as the light turns golden. Stop somewhere pretty for a drink and nibbles by the canals.
EveningBack to the villa for a home-cooked dinner and an early night. We've earned it. 🌙
Annecy Old Town — our first evening walk
2
Tue 23 June

Lake Day

Beaches, kayaks, books, the clearest water

Lake
MorningSlow breakfast on the terrace. Espresso, fruit, mountains. No rush whatsoever.
Late MornMake sandwiches from the food shop — pack a picnic bag, towels, sun cream, a book each.
AfternoonHead to one of the lake beaches. Swim in the clearest water in Europe, lay out a blanket, eat lunch, read. Rent kayaks if we feel like an adventure.
EveningBack to the villa for movie night — Marshall speaker on, something good on the smart TV, cosy after a day in the sun. 🎬
Lake Annecy — turquoise and impossibly clear
3
Wed 24 June

Cycle the Lake & Sunset Picnic

35km around the shore, then Col de la Forclaz

Active
EarlyEarly start. Rent bikes and set off on the lakeside cycle path — flat, traffic-free, lake on one side, Alps on the other.
MorningStop at the cute little villages along the way for coffee breaks. Take it slow, no agenda.
MiddayRoll into Talloires — the most beautiful village on the lake. Stone buildings, flower boxes, boats on the water. Lunch in the village.
AfternoonCycle back, return the bikes, head to the villa for a rest and a pool dip.
SunsetDrive up to Col de la Forclaz with a picnic. Watch the sun drop behind the mountains from the legendary viewpoint above the lake. One of the most beautiful views in France. 🌅
Col de la Forclaz — our sunset picnic spot
4
Thu 25 June

Slow Morning & Alpine Hike

Rest, recover, then the mountains on foot

Relaxed
MorningA properly slow one. Sleep in. Pool. Read by the terrace. Recover from yesterday's legs. No plans before lunch.
AfternoonHead out on an alpine hiking route in the hills above Annecy. Wildflower meadows, panoramic views down to the turquoise lake, maybe even a chamois sighting.
EveningBack into Annecy Old Town for the evening. Wander the medieval streets, find somewhere lovely for dinner, sorbet by the canals. ✨
Alpine trails — wildflowers & lake views
